# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- ⌘⇥ goes through your apps in Dock order: Finder, the apps kept in your Dock, then other open apps.
- While holding ⌘: Tab / ⇧Tab or ←/→ to move, ⌘1…⌘0 to jump to a position, Q to quit and
  H to hide the selected app, Esc to cancel. Hover and click work too.
- Position numbers as a ⌘-badge on each icon, a row above or below the icons, hidden, or off.
- Optionally shows Dock apps that aren't running, so positions never shift.
- Choose whether the first ⌘⇥ selects the next app or always the first app in the Dock.
- Open at Login, English and German, light and dark mode.
- The built-in ⌘⇥ comes back whenever Dockstep quits or crashes.
- Runs on macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
